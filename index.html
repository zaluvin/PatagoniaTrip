Some journeys are made to last.

Seven days between Chile and Argentina. An invitation-only expedition, departing from Brasília.

A well-made object

In the 1960s, one car managed to be beautiful, fast and fair all at once, without ever raising its voice. This journey comes from the same spirit: proportion, performance, and the certainty that the essentials were done right.

We handle it

Flights, roads, border, lodging and paperwork stay with us.

You walk

Guided trails among glaciers, lakes and mountains, at the pace of someone who loves to walk.

You photograph

Every stage is planned around the light and the record. What you bring home is an archive.

The route

From the far south northward, never retracing the same road. Each milestone is a pause on the way.

  1. IDay I

    Punta Arenas, Chile

    Arrival at the airport and the start of the journey. The flight is already part of the trip.

  2. IIDay I, night

    Puerto Natales, Chile

    About three hours by road from the airport. The first night, with the fjord at the door.

  3. IIIDays II to IV

    EcoCamp Patagonia, Torres del Paine

    Our base in domes, at the foot of the mountains. From here we walk the French Valley and the Base of the Towers, both out-and-back, with Lago Azul as the alternative when the weather asks for it.

  4. IVDay V

    El Calafate, Argentina

    The border crossing in the morning and a free afternoon by the lake.

  5. VDay VI, morning

    Perito Moreno Glacier, Argentina

    The ice wall seen up close, from the walkways and from a boat.

  6. VIDays VI and VII

    El Chaltén, Argentina

    Rivers, forests and the Fitz Roy trails, with sunrise on the granite spires.

  7. VIIDeparture

    El Calafate Airport, Argentina

    A morning of farewell, about 21 km from the city, and the flight home.

The archive

Every place yields a series. In the end, you return with a collection of your own.
